        Sources. Every trophy's guide names its sources beside it: the official list on Exophase for its name, description and grade; PSNProfiles for the PSN progress target where one exists; and this guide's own advice, written from the official description. A specific location or step with no cited source earns no credit in this guide — and where a place or step has not been verified in the game yet, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing it.
